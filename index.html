SciForce

Workforce, contractor, attendance and visitor management for Scimax Agrosciences Pvt. Ltd., Halol plant.

What SciForce does

SciForce is an internal Android app used by Scimax Agrosciences HR, security staff, labour contractors and security agencies to register workers, record gate attendance, issue visitor gate passes and produce reports.

Who can use it

Only people approved by Scimax HR or the Super Admin can see any data. Signing in with Google only identifies the person; access is granted inside the app.
