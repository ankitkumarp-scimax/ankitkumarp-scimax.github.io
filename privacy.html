SciForce privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

SciForce is an internal app of Scimax Agrosciences Pvt. Ltd. (Halol plant, Gujarat, India) for managing plant workforce, contractors, security guards, attendance and visitors. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is protected.

Information we collect

  • App users (HR, security staff, contractors, security agencies): name, email address from Google sign-in, mobile number, role and organisation.
  • Workers and guards registered in the app: name, date of birth, gender, mobile and emergency contact, passport-size photograph, Aadhaar number and Aadhaar card images, contractor or agency, and attendance (gate entry and exit times).
  • Visitors: name, mobile, company, purpose, person to meet, photo and ID type with only the last digits of the ID number.
  • Device information and an audit log of actions performed in the app, for security.

Google sign-in

When you choose "Continue with Google", SciForce receives only your name, email address and Google account ID. It does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Signing in does not give access to any information until Scimax HR or the Super Admin approves your account.

How we use the information

Only to run plant operations: worker registration and approval, statutory and safety records, gate attendance, visitor gate passes, labour requirement tracking, reports and birthday greetings. The information is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

How it is protected

  • Data is stored with Supabase in the Mumbai (India) region and sent only over encrypted connections.
  • Aadhaar numbers are encrypted in the database. Screens, reports and notifications show only a masked number (last four digits). Full numbers can be viewed only by authorised HR, and every view is logged.
  • Aadhaar images and photos are kept in private storage, reachable only by authorised users through short-lived links.
  • Each role sees only what it needs: a contractor sees only its own workers, a security agency only its own guards.

Sharing

Information is shared only within Scimax Agrosciences and, for their own workers, with the respective contractor or agency, or when required by law or a government authority.

Retention and your choices

Records are kept while the person works at or visits the plant and afterwards for as long as required by labour and factory laws. You may ask to see or correct your information, or ask about deletion, by writing to the contact below.
